Three steps, and the second one is not yours

  1. 01

    Record where you already are

    Press record in the menu bar for Zoom, Meet, Teams or a room with a laptop on the table. Nothing joins the call, so nobody has to admit a stranger.

  2. 02

    NovaMind reads the whole conversation

    It listens for the moments a group commits: "let's go with", "can you own", "by Friday". Small talk and status updates stay out of the summary.

  3. 03

    One page goes to everyone who was there

    Decisions first, then actions with owners and dates, then the questions nobody answered. Each line links to the second of audio it came from.
